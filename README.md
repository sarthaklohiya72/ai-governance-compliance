# AI Governance & Compliance — Daily Learning Log

A daily-updated series covering the frameworks, regulations, and practices that
govern how AI systems are built, deployed, and monitored responsibly.

One new post is added on a regular cadence, working through the curriculum below
in order. Each post is a short, practical explainer — what the framework says,
who it applies to, and what it actually requires in practice.

> **Disclaimer:** This repo is an educational learning log, not legal advice.
> Regulations referenced here change frequently — always verify current
> requirements with primary sources or qualified legal counsel before making
> compliance decisions.

## How this works

- Posts live in [`posts/`](./posts), numbered in curriculum order.
- Each post is researched fresh at the time it's written (regulations move fast —
  dates and requirements below reflect the most recent research at time of posting).
- The table below tracks progress through the curriculum.
